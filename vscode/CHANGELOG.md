# Changelog

## 0.1.0

First public preview.

- Plays the active file, the selection, or from the cursor, with the editor highlighting the playing character.
- The player lives in a bottom-panel tab (draggable to a side bar), with a compact layout for narrow bars and a mini player for short panels.
- Follows the editor's theme by default (`radio.theme`: `editor` or `radio`).
- All 13 rad.io styles and controls (key, scale, tempo, background voice, layers, seed).
